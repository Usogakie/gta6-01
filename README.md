Palmera Bay is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser: explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Palmera Bay PD under a five-star wanted system, fight rival gangs, and play through a five-mission story from broke nobody to Bay King — with traffic, pedestrians, a live minimap, combat, synthesized sound and save progress.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press ENTER to start. WASD moves and drives, E enters a car, Space shoots, F punches, Shift runs, P pauses, M mutes.
