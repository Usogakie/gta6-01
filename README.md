Palmera Bay is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser: explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Palmera Bay PD under a five-star wanted system, fight rival gangs, and play a five-mission story from broke nobody to Bay King — with traffic, pedestrians, a live minimap, combat, synthesised sound and saved progress.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080/ and press Enter to start. Move with WASD, press E to enter a car, Space to shoot, F to punch, Shift to run, P to pause and M to mute.
